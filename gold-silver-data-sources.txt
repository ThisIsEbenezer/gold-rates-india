SAM'S GOLD PREDICTION - WHERE THE PRICES COME FROM
====================================================

Short answer
------------
The site does NOT read the shop (jeweller) rate from anywhere. It takes the
global gold and silver price in US dollars, converts it to rupees, adds
Indian import duty and a fixed shop margin ("premium"), and rounds it the
way Chennai shops do. So the numbers are an estimate of the shop price,
usually within about 0.5% to 1%, not the exact shop price.


1. LIVE PRICE SOURCES (all free, no API key)
--------------------------------------------
Gold price in USD per ounce (XAU)
  Main:     https://api.gold-api.com/price/XAU
  Backup:   Yahoo Finance gold futures (GC=F)

Silver price in USD per ounce (XAG)
  Main:     https://api.gold-api.com/price/XAG
  Backup:   Yahoo Finance silver futures (SI=F)

US dollar to Indian rupee (USD/INR)
  Main:     https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD
  Backup 1: https://api.frankfurter.dev/v1/latest?base=USD&symbols=INR
  Backup 2: Yahoo Finance (INR=X)

If a main source fails, the site tries the next one. If all fail, it shows
the last saved price and a warning.


2. HISTORY AND TOMORROW'S ESTIMATE SOURCE
-----------------------------------------
Yahoo Finance daily prices (query1.finance.yahoo.com, chart data) for gold,
silver and USD/INR. Used for:
  - the "Last 30 days" chart (older days)
  - the volatility and momentum used in "Tomorrow's estimate"

Tomorrow's estimate is a statistical guess (recent daily volatility plus a
small amount of recent momentum). It is not advice and cannot be exact.


3. HOW THE RUPEE PRICE IS CALCULATED
------------------------------------
  24K price per gram (Rs) =
      gold USD per ounce / 31.1034768      (ounce to grams)
      x USD/INR
      x 1.15                               (15% import duty)
      x (1 + premium)                      (shop margin)

  Silver uses the same steps with the silver price and its own premium.

Import duty: 15% (10% basic duty + 5% cess), in force since 13 May 2026.
  Can be changed in Netlify with IMPORT_DUTY_PCT.
Premium (default): gold 2.1%, silver 17%.
  Can be changed in Netlify with LOCAL_PREMIUM_PCT and SILVER_PREMIUM_PCT.
  These were measured by comparing against real Chennai shop prices on
  25 and 26 Nov 2026. Shop margins drift a little every day, so a small
  difference from the shop is normal. Silver differs the most.
GST (+3%) is not in the price. It is shown only when you switch it on.


4. HOW IT IS ROUNDED (to match Chennai shops)
---------------------------------------------
  - 22K is rounded to the nearest Rs 5 per gram.
  - 24K = 22K x 24/22 and 18K = 22K x 18/22, each rounded to a whole rupee.
  - Prices are rounded per gram first, then multiplied by the weight
    (so 10 g = 10 x the one-gram price).
This reproduces the shop tables you sent on 25 and 26 Nov 2026.


5. HOW OFTEN IT UPDATES
-----------------------
  - The page asks for fresh prices every 30 seconds.
  - A background job runs every 10 minutes to record prices, even if nobody
    has the page open. This builds "Today's price changes" and the
    "Live today" graph (about one point every 5 minutes).
  - Weekends: global gold and silver trading is closed from Saturday
    03:30 AM IST to Monday 04:30 AM IST (winter time), so prices hold.
  - Morning rate and evening rate are saved once a day at 9:30 AM and
    3:30 PM IST (the times the Chennai jewellers' association publishes).
    They are used for the track record, not shown on the page.


6. WHERE DATA IS STORED
-----------------------
Netlify Blobs (built into your Netlify site): today's price changes, the
live graph points, daily closes, and past estimates for the track record.
Nothing is stored about visitors.


7. WHAT THE SITE DOES NOT USE
-----------------------------
  - No MJDTA (Chennai association) feed. Their price API needs a paid
    licence from Logimax, so the site cannot read the exact shop rate.
  - No IBJA feed.
  - No jeweller websites or scraping.


8. LIMITS TO KNOW
-----------------
  - Estimate, not the shop's own rate: expect about 0.5% to 1% difference,
    more for silver.
  - Shops fix their rate about twice a day; the site moves with the global
    market all day, so the two will differ between shop updates.
  - Free data sources can be delayed or briefly unavailable.
  - Estimates are not investment advice.
