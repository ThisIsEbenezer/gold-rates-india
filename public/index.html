How weekend and Monday rates work

Do Friday night’s rates carry over to Saturday and Sunday?
Yes. Indian bullion exchanges (MCX) trade Monday to Friday only, and global gold trading stops on Friday at 5 PM New York time. That is Saturday 3:30 AM IST in winter (2:30 AM in US summer time). Between the Friday evening close in India and that moment the global price can still move a little, so the final Friday rate is the one that holds all weekend.
Do Indian rates follow only the US price?
Not only. The base is the international spot price in US dollars (London and New York set it, Asia trades it too), converted at the USD/INR rate, then import duty is added. Since 13 May 2026 that duty is 15% on gold and silver, and 3% GST applies when you buy. Your jeweller then adds a local premium or making charge.
If something happens in the US on Saturday or Sunday, when does India see it?
Nothing trades on the weekend, so the news shows up as a price gap when global trading reopens on Sunday at 6 PM New York time. That is Monday 4:30 AM IST in winter, which is before Indian markets open at 9 AM. Monday’s first rate in India therefore already contains the weekend news.
How is tomorrow’s estimate made?
From recent daily closes: current volatility sets the range, and a small share of recent momentum nudges the middle. Gold and silver are very hard to predict one day ahead, so read the range, not the single number. It is not investment advice.

Indicative rates. Calculated from international spot prices, the USD/INR rate and India’s import duty. Actual jeweller, city and bank rates differ because of local premiums, making charges and taxes. Exchange holidays are not modelled.
